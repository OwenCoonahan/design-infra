# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively; welcome different backgrounds and skill levels. Respect privacy, attribution and other people's work.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, spam and unwelcome sexual attention are not permitted in repository discussions or community spaces. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or remove access for violations, proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer through an existing direct channel. Do not publish personal details in an issue. If no private channel is available, ask a maintainer for one without describing the incident publicly. Reports will be handled discreetly; retaliation is not permitted.
